About Us

From a Kitchen Combi to a Full Plant Room

We're a London based firm working out of Bedfont, and we've been a registered limited company since 2015. We fit, fix and service boilers in people's homes, and we install commercial and industrial boilers along with all the plant and pipework that goes with them. Same standards whether it's a combi in a semi or a plant room under an office block.

Every gas job we do is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers, to current Gas Safety Regulations. No shortcuts on gas, ever.
